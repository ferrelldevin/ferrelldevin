### Work

Each project has a full case study on the site: the problem, what I built, and how it was checked.

- **[Veteran Benefits Kit](https://devinferrell.com/work/veteran-benefits-kit/):** pay planner, benefit finder and front-desk map for ODU veteran students, built alongside campus benefits staff. Every figure is cited to VA or ODU.
- **[Kurikaeshi](https://devinferrell.com/work/kurikaeshi/):** spaced-repetition app for ODU Japanese courses, with free accounts, course decks and a page for instructors.
- **[VA Chapter 31 worksheet](https://devinferrell.com/work/va-chapter-31-worksheet/):** turning certified credit hours into VA training time and pay, cited to 38 CFR, including the independent review that failed it. Code: [va-chapter31-worksheet](https://github.com/ferrelldevin/va-chapter31-worksheet)
- **[resume-gate](https://devinferrell.com/work/resume-gate/):** a resume generator with a gate for the claims it can check mechanically, and a record of what that gate missed. Code: [resume-gate](https://github.com/ferrelldevin/resume-gate)
- **[Study Kit](https://devinferrell.com/work/study-kit/):** fact-checked math cheat sheets from precalculus through Calc II, plus Fall 2026 study sheets for ODU Japanese courses.

### How I work

- Primary sources over summaries. If a number comes from a regulation, the page cites the regulation.

- AI as a tool, checked like any other. I build with AI assistants every day, and I build the checks that catch their mistakes.
